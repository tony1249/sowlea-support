常見問題

基本功能需要付款或登入嗎？

一般節拍器與調音器可免費使用，不需要建立 Sowlea 帳號。目前版本沒有廣告。iOS 首版付費方案只提供可選的 Sowlea Plus 一次買斷，沒有訂閱或自動續費；以 Apple 購買畫面顯示的價格及可用狀態為準。

如何恢復 Plus 購買？

確認裝置使用原本購買時的 Apple 帳號，連上網路，開啟 Sowlea 的「設定」→「恢復購買」。如果沒有恢復，請聯絡客服並描述畫面訊息，不要提供帳號密碼。TestFlight 沙盒測試權益不等於正式 App Store 的付款紀錄。

調音器沒有反應？

請主動開始調音並允許麥克風權限。若曾拒絕，可到 iOS「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「麥克風」檢查 Sowlea 權限。讓樂器靠近麥克風、減少環境噪音，並確認選取正確樂器與調弦。拒絕麥克風權限不影響一般節拍器。

錄音保存在哪裡？

快速錄音只保留最近成功保存的一段於裝置本機，新錄音成功保存後會取代上一段。可在 App 設定中選擇「刪除最近錄音」。移除 App 或清除資料可能使錄音與設定消失，請先保存你需要的內容。調音功能本身不保存錄音。

遇到購買或退款問題？

付款及退款由 Apple 處理。可前往 Apple「回報問題」查看可用選項；是否符合退款資格由 Apple 判定。

如何回報 TestFlight 問題？

可在 TestFlight 中提交回饋，或透過上方信箱聯絡我們。請註明建置版本；測試版的功能與正式版可能不同。

Common questions

Do basic features require payment or sign-in?

Basic metronome and tuner features are free and do not require a Sowlea account. The current app has no ads. The first iOS release is planned to offer only optional Sowlea Plus as a one-time purchase, without subscriptions or automatic renewal. Refer to Apple's purchase sheet for the actual price and availability.

How do I restore Plus?

Use the Apple Account that made the original purchase, connect to the internet, then open Sowlea Settings → Restore Purchases. If restoration fails, contact support with the message you see, never your password. TestFlight sandbox entitlements are not paid App Store purchase records.

Why is the tuner not responding?

Start tuning and allow microphone access. If previously denied, check Sowlea under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. Move the instrument closer, reduce background noise, and select the correct instrument and tuning. Basic metronome use does not require microphone access.

Where is my recording stored?

Quick recording retains only the latest successfully saved take on your device. A new successful take replaces the previous one. Use Delete Latest Recording in app settings to remove it. Removing the app or clearing data may erase recordings and settings; save anything you need first. The tuner itself does not save recordings.

What about payments and refunds?

Apple handles payment and refund requests. Visit Apple's Report a Problem for available options. Apple determines refund eligibility.

How do I report a TestFlight issue?

Send feedback in TestFlight or email the address above. Include the build number. Beta features may differ from the public release.
